# Amentys Trademarks and Branding

## Protected names

`Amentys`, the Amentys logo, and the names used for official Amentys subsystems may identify
the official project. Copyright and trademark rights are separate from the software license.
The ASAL does not grant permission to use these names or logos for a modified, unofficial,
or commercial distribution.

## Permitted descriptive use

You may truthfully state that your work is compatible with, derived from, or discusses Amentys,
provided that the statement does not suggest endorsement, certification, authorship, or official
status.

Do not use Amentys names or logos in a way that could confuse users about the origin of a
modified product. Do not register confusingly similar domain names, package names, company
names, or social-media identities.

## Official status

Only the rights holder or an explicitly authorized maintainer may describe a release, build,
hardware device, or service as official, certified, or endorsed by Amentys.

Questions about branding should be raised through the official repository before publication.
