# Amentys Code of Conduct

## Our Commitments

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we, as contributors and maintainers of the Amentys project, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards – Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment:

Showing empathy and kindness toward other members.

Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences; accepting constructive criticism graciously.

Focusing on what is best for the community and the Amentys monolithic philosophy (unifying our strengths rather than fragmenting the project).

Encouraging mutual assistance and patiently guiding new contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviors

The use of sexual language or imagery, as well as sexual attention or advances of any kind.

Belittling comments, personal attacks, insults, or harassment (trolling).

Public or private harassment.

Publishing others private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission (doxxing).

Any other behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainers Responsibilities

Project maintainers (the Original Author, Willy Micieli, and the designated team) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, or to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Enforcement of the Code of Conduct

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the Original Author directly at: saigoekitae@gmail.com.

All complaints will be reviewed and addressed promptly and confidentially.

Community members who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith risk temporary or permanent exclusion from Amentys community spaces.
